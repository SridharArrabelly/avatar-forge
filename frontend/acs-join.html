🎙️ Bring {{AVATAR_NAME}} into a Teams meeting

This page joins a Microsoft Teams meeting as an audio assistant using Azure Communication Services. Once joined, anyone in the call can say a wake phrase (e.g. "Hey {{AVATAR_NAME}}") and ask a question aloud; {{AVATAR_NAME}} answers by voice, grounded in the knowledge base.

Please notify participants: an AI assistant is present and listening in the meeting. {{AVATAR_NAME}} does not record or transcribe the meeting — she only listens live to answer when addressed.

Hear remote participants: by default {{AVATAR_NAME}} hears only this device's microphone (Teams isolates per-client audio). To let her hear the far side, click Capture far-side audio and share the Teams window/tab with “Share audio” ticked.

This runs the same capture pipeline as the web app — same microphone settings, same 40 ms AudioWorklet, same Voice Live turn detection. The one difference is that a meeting also needs a room tap to hear other participants, and nothing echo-cancels that tap. While {{AVATAR_NAME}} speaks it carries her own voice back, so it stays closed either way. Ticking the box keeps only the microphone open, which is exactly what the web app does. Use headphones — on speakers her voice returns through the mic instead.
